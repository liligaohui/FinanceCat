How to add cat photos 1. Put your photo files (JPG, PNG, WEBP) into the cats/ folder in your GitHub repo.
2. Open cats/index.json and add each filename to the array:
    ["sleeping-on-textbook.jpg", "monday-morning.jpg", "exam-panic.png"]
3. Push to GitHub — photos appear here and on the homepage automatically.

Tip: Name your files descriptively with hyphens — the filename becomes the photo caption automatically.
